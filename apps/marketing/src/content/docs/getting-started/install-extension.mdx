# Install the Browser Extension

The Bittery browser extension lets you autofill logins, save new passwords, and generate strong credentials — all without leaving the page.

## Supported browsers

- **Google Chrome** (and Chromium-based browsers)
- **Microsoft Edge**
- **Brave**

<Callout type="info">
Firefox and Safari support are still on the roadmap.
</Callout>

## Installation

<Steps>
<Step title="Download the extension">
Visit the [Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore) and search for **Bittery Password Manager**, or use the direct link from your Bittery dashboard.
</Step>
<Step title="Click 'Add to Chrome'">
Confirm the permissions prompt. Bittery only requests the minimum permissions needed for autofill.
</Step>
<Step title="Sign in">
Click the Bittery icon in your browser toolbar and enter your email, master password, and Secret Key.
</Step>
<Step title="Pin the extension">
For easy access, click the puzzle icon in your toolbar and pin Bittery.
</Step>
</Steps>

## How autofill works

When you visit a login page, Bittery detects the form fields and offers to fill your saved credentials. You can:

- **Click** the Bittery icon in the input field
- **Use the keyboard shortcut** `Ctrl+Shift+L` (or `Cmd+Shift+L` on Mac)
- **Right-click** and select "Bittery → Autofill"

<Callout type="tip">
The extension works entirely offline after the initial sync. Your passwords are decrypted locally — nothing is sent to our servers during autofill.
</Callout>

## Saving new passwords

When you sign up for a new site or change a password, Bittery will prompt you to save the credentials. You can also:

1. Open the extension popup
2. Click **+ New Item**
3. Fill in the details manually

## Troubleshooting

If autofill isn't working on a specific site:

- Make sure the extension is enabled and you're signed in
- Try refreshing the page
- Some sites use non-standard form fields — use the extension popup to copy and paste instead
