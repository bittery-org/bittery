# Create Your Account

Getting started with Bittery takes less than two minutes. Your account is secured from the very first step — we never see your password or encryption keys.

## What you'll need

- An email address
- A strong master password (we recommend 14+ characters)

## Sign up

<Steps>
<Step title="Go to the sign-up page">
Visit [app.bittery.com](https://app.bittery.com) and click **Create Account**.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter your email">
Provide a valid email address. This is used as part of your encryption salt and for account recovery.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a master password">
Pick a strong, memorable password. This is the only password you'll ever need to remember. Bittery will never store or transmit it.
</Step>
<Step title="Save your Secret Key">
After signing up, you'll receive a **Secret Key** in the format `A3-XXXXXX-XXXXXX-...`. Write this down and store it somewhere safe — it's required to sign in on new devices.

<Callout type="warning">
Your Secret Key cannot be recovered if lost. Store it in a secure physical location.
</Callout>
</Step>
<Step title="You're in!">
Your vault is created and encrypted. Start adding passwords, secure notes, and more.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Understanding your credentials

Bittery uses a **dual-key architecture** for maximum security:

| Credential | Purpose | Can be changed? |
|-----------|---------|----------------|
| **Master Password** | Unlocks your vault on trusted devices | Yes |
| **Secret Key** | Required for new device sign-ins | Yes (regenerate) |
| **Email** | Account identifier and encryption salt | Yes |

Together, these derive your **Master Unlock Key** via PBKDF2 (600,000 iterations), which encrypts and decrypts everything in your vault.
