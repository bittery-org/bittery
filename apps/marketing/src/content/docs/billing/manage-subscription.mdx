# Manage Your Subscription

You can manage your Bittery subscription at any time from your account settings. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

## Upgrading your plan

<Steps>
<Step title="Go to Settings → Billing">
Open the Bittery web app and navigate to your billing settings.
</Step>
<Step title="Select a new plan">
Choose the plan you'd like to upgrade to. You'll see a prorated cost for the remainder of your current billing period.
</Step>
<Step title="Confirm payment">
Review the charges and confirm. Your new plan features are available immediately.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Downgrading your plan

When downgrading:

- Your current plan remains active until the end of the billing period
- Features exclusive to your current plan will become unavailable at the end of the period
- Your data is never deleted — vault items remain encrypted and accessible
- If you exceed the new plan's limits, you'll be prompted to adjust before the downgrade takes effect

## Canceling your subscription

If you cancel your paid subscription:

1. Your account reverts to the **Free plan** at the end of the billing period
2. All your encrypted data remains intact and accessible
3. Paid features (sharing, TOTP, etc.) become unavailable
4. You can resubscribe at any time

<Callout type="info">
Canceling your subscription never deletes your data. Your vault, passwords, and secure notes remain encrypted and accessible on the Free plan.
</Callout>

## Payment methods

Bittery accepts:

- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
- PayPal (where available)

## Billing questions

- **Invoices** are available in Settings → Billing → Invoice History
- **Payment failures** — you'll receive an email notification and have 7 days to update your payment method
- **Refunds** — contact support within 30 days of a charge for a full refund
