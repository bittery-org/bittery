# Plans & Pricing

Bittery offers a generous **free plan** and affordable paid plans for individuals, families, and teams.

## Available plans

| Plan | Price | Best for |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Free** | $0/month | Individuals getting started |
| **Personal** | $3/month | Power users who want full features |
| **Family** | $7/month | Families (up to 6 members) |
| **Team** | $6/user/month | Businesses and organizations |

## What's included in every plan

Every Bittery plan includes:

- Unlimited password storage
- AES-256-GCM encryption
- Cross-device sync
- Browser extension
- Password generator
- Secure notes

## Paid plan extras

Upgrading to a paid plan unlocks:

- **TOTP authenticator** — store 2FA codes alongside your passwords
- **Vault sharing** — securely share individual items or entire vaults
- **Priority support** — faster response times
- **Advanced item types** — credit cards, identities
- **File attachments** — attach files to vault items (encrypted)

<Callout type="info">
All plans use the exact same encryption and security model. We never compromise on security based on plan tier.
</Callout>

## Self-hosted option

Bittery is open-source. You can self-host the entire stack for free with no feature limitations. Self-hosting gives you full control over your data and infrastructure.

Visit [github.com/bittery-org/bittery](https://github.com/bittery-org/bittery) for self-hosting documentation.

## Annual billing

Save **20%** by choosing annual billing on any paid plan. You can switch between monthly and annual billing at any time from your account settings.
