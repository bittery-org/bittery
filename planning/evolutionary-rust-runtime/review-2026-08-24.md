## Reviewer claims checked

The Standards review found no hard documented-standard breach. Its source-text-test claims are real:
`runtime-session.test.ts` and parts of `runtime-items.test.ts` match implementation identifiers and
hand-picked files. They do not replace behavioral coverage. The ownership blind spot is already
closed by `web-import-graph.ts` plus `transitional-reachability.test.ts`; the missing product-path
coverage is captured by ticket 32. Duplicated generator and existing-host lifecycle code are
non-blocking design judgements outside this slice.

The Spec review's SQLite/conformance and browser-acceptance claims were confirmed by repository-wide
searches, Cargo manifests, the existing adapter tests, the E2E inventory, and ticket 21's own comment
that its full scenario was not yet asserted.
