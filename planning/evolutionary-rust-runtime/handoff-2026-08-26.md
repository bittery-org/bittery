## Ticket 48 decision and split

The maintainer accepted the recommended destructive contract. This answer is recorded in English in
Ticket 48:

- `RemoveAccount { accountId }` and `Wipe` are explicit irreversible local-destruction authority.
- Core fences selected runners and plaintext/key leases first. Teardown may then destroy pending
  accepted Operations in the requested scope; this is not per-Operation discard.
- The outcome names the explicit Account or Device scope and is `complete` only if every required
  phase is proven complete. Otherwise it is `incomplete` with bounded redacted phase names.
- An identical explicit retry must converge without consulting an active Account.

Ticket 48 is split into four sequential slices. This split and the binding decision are already in
`48-runtime-account-removal-and-wipe.md`.
