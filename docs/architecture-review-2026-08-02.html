Problem

ICrypto is the declared seam but not the real one: it carries optional members, unions sync with async (boolean | Promise<boolean>), and platform-context.tsx:70 instructs callers to import * as crypto straight from the platform module. The four adapters have drifted in surface, naming, and coverage, and nothing tests them against a shared contract.

Solution

Wins

· leverage: one interface, four platforms
· 28 web-only operations become portable
· crypto gets its first cross-adapter tests
Precedent, not conflict: packages/storage/CONTEXT.md §2 already states the rule this candidate applies — "every method is total, so the compiler verifies that an adapter satisfies the contract". Crypto is the package that never got it.

The first real decision

01 — Unlock has three implementations

00 comes before it in time but not in importance — it is cleanup, it asks nothing of you, and it makes this diff smaller.

It is the smallest change with the largest effect, and it is already half done. Two entry points on unlock.ts exist, are tested, and have zero callers; two hooks reimplement exactly what they do. Routing the hooks through them is mostly deletion — no new seam, no new module, no port to design.

It also sits where the work is: five of the last eight commits touch unlock, session, and account lifecycle, and every one of them had to be reasoned about three times. Closing that first makes candidates 02 and 03 — which both reshape what unlock stands on — a single-caller change rather than a three-caller one.
