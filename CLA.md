## Why This Agreement Exists

Bittery is distributed under the GNU AGPLv3 (server) and GNU GPLv3 (clients, crypto, and packages). We also distribute our mobile clients through platform app stores — notably Apple's App Store — whose terms of service impose usage restrictions that conflict with GPLv3 (see GPLv3 §7 and the anti-tivoization provisions). VLC was removed from the App Store in 2011 over precisely this conflict.

Section 3 of this Agreement grants us the relicensing rights that let us distribute your Contribution through those channels lawfully. Without it, a single contributor could later object to App Store distribution and we would have no recourse — which would mean pulling our iOS and Android apps.

This is the entire reason the Agreement exists. We are not asking for these rights in order to take the Project proprietary; see "Our Commitment To You" below.

## How To Sign

You do not need to email anything. When you open your first pull request, the CLA Assistant bot will comment with a link. Signing is a one-time action performed by clicking through that link with your GitHub account; it covers all of your future contributions to this repository.

Your GitHub username, the timestamp, and the fact that you signed are recorded in a public file in this organization. Do not include a postal address or any other personal information in a pull request in order to sign.

If you are contributing on behalf of your employer, and your employer claims rights to work you create, your employer must sign a Corporate CLA before your contributions can be merged. Contact `legal@bittery.com`.
